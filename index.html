Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Still unsure? Send a message and ask before committing to anything.

What does the one-month Nitro offer cover?

It is the agreed payment for the development service. The exact server tasks and deliverables are confirmed with you in Discord before work starts.

Can you set up an entire server?

Yes. We can discuss a full structure including categories, channels, roles, permissions, moderation and community bots.

Do I need to give you my password?

No. Never share your Discord password or account login. Any required access should be provided through Discord's normal server roles and permissions.

Is Discord Develop officially connected to Discord?

No. Discord Develop is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Discord Inc.
